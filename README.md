# CGS 1700 Midterm

**Take the exam here: https://kinglyonz.github.io/cgs1700-midterm/**

The exam has two parts, 150 points.

1. Open the link and enter your first name, last name and school email.
2. Start each part only when your instructor says so. Part 1 is 60 minutes. Part 2 is 40 minutes.
3. When you submit a part, your answers lock. You can review them, but you cannot change them.
4. Email each part to your instructor from your school email. The page gives you the email.
5. Attach your terminal screenshot to the Part 2 email.

Both emails are due by 8:00 PM. Your first email for each part is final.
